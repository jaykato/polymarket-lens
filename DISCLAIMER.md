Nothing produced by this software is investment, financial, legal, or tax

The application shows observations, not forecasts:

- **Market conditions** describe how expensive a market is to enter and exit.
  A market in good condition is not a market that will be profitable.
- **Price movement** summarises what has already been recorded. Past movement
  says nothing about future movement.
- **"Crowd leans Yes / No"** restates what current prices imply about other
  participants. It is not this application's own view.

## Data accuracy

All data comes from Polymarket's public APIs and is stored locally as fetched.
It may be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. Synchronisation is manual, so figures
on screen can be hours or days old. Never treat anything shown here as
authoritative. Verify against the source before acting on it.
